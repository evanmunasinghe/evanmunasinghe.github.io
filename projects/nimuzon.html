Nimuzon.com

An online marketplace for natural Sri Lankan gemstones such as Ceylon sapphires and padparadscha, where buyers compare certified stones and contact verified sellers directly.

  • Marketplace
  • Live auctions
  • Multi-currency

Visit nimuzon.com

The problem

Buying a gemstone online depends on trust. Buyers need to see the stone, check its certificate and know who is selling it. Sellers need a way to reach buyers worldwide without giving up a share of every sale.

What it does

  • Shop by gemstone, shape and origin, with photos, video and full specifications for each stone.
  • Lab certificate uploads with treatments disclosed, so buyers can check any report themselves.
  • Live auctions with open bidding and an alert the moment you are outbid.
  • Verified seller shops with reviews and direct contact details. Sellers keep every sale.
  • Prices shown in ten currencies, including LKR, USD, EUR and GBP.
  • Gem names shown in English and Sinhala.
