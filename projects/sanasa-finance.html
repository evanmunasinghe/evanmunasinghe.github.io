Sanasa Finance Suite

A modern replacement for a Sanasa cooperative's outdated system, with a new interface and new features covering members, loans, collections, deductions, accounting and printable passbooks.

  • .NET
  • SQL Server
  • Front end
  • Print layouts

The problem

Sanasa was running its members' savings and loans on an old, outdated system. Staff needed something quicker to use at the counter that still kept the books right and printed the paper records members carry, like their passbooks.

What it does

  • Members: a directory searchable by member ID, NIC or pension number, and member registration.
  • Loans: applications, approval status, loan collections and interest calculation.
  • AGO deductions: deduction setups and monthly processing.
  • Transactions: receipts, payment vouchers, receivables and a cheque manager.
  • Accounting: general ledger, cash book and bank book, with balances on the dashboard.
  • A dashboard with members, outstanding loans, today's collections and liquidity at a glance.
  • An audit trail of system actions, role-based users and a language switcher.
  • Printable layouts for passbooks and other documents.

My role

At Eyeplex Solution, from November 2025 to July 2026, I helped replace Sanasa's old system with this suite, built on .NET and Microsoft SQL Server, giving it a new interface and adding new features. I worked mostly on the front end: the dashboard and module screens, and the printable layouts for passbooks and other documents. I also installed and set up the system on a local machine at Sanasa, where it runs today.
