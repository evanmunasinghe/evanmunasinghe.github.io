FleeV

Cloud garage management for independent auto repair shops, replacing paper work orders, missed bookings and scattered records with one dashboard that works in any browser.

  • Laravel
  • MySQL
  • Cloud SaaS

Visit fleev.net

The problem

Many independent garages run on paper job sheets, messages and phone calls. Work orders get lost, bookings are missed, invoices are written by hand, and a customer's history lives only in someone's memory.

What it does

  • Bookings, work orders and invoicing in one place, from first booking to paid invoice.
  • Customer and vehicle history that anyone in the shop can look up.
  • Parts and stock tracking alongside the jobs that use them.
  • Team chat for the people working in the shop.
  • A customer app and a garage finder for car owners.
  • Runs in the browser on any device, with no installation.

My role

I created FleeV at Virtual System Solutions as an add-on for the Ant ERP. It connects through the Ant ERP API v1, which I also built.
