The problem

Small businesses often run sales on one system, stock in a notebook and the books with an accountant at month end. Invotron puts all three in one place: ring up a sale, and the stock count and the books are already done.

What it does

  • Sets itself up for the business type chosen at sign-up: retail shops, electronics and appliances, salons, clinics and repair shops, or restaurants and cafes.
  • Staff can use it in English, Sinhala or Tamil.
  • A Windows till app keeps selling when the internet is down.
  • Product variants, barcode labels and scanning, and expiry dates for food, medicine and cosmetics.
  • Quotations, A4 invoices, returns and exchanges, plus warranties and serial-number tracking for repairs.
  • Payroll, VAT and full double-entry accounting, so the books are always up to date.
  • Move from an old POS with a spreadsheet: every row is checked before anything is saved, and re-uploading the same file never creates duplicates.
