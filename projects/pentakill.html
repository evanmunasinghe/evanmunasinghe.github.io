Pentakill

Look up any League of Legends player, count every pentakill in their match history, and compare two players side by side.

  • Riot Games API
  • UI design

Visit the app

The problem

The game shows a pentakill when it happens, but nowhere tells you how many you have had in total. Finding out means scrolling through match after match.

What it does

  • Search by Riot ID (in-game name plus tag, like Hide on bush#KR1). The region is found for you.
  • Goes through the player's match history and counts every pentakill.
  • Compare mode puts two players side by side.
  • Champion art comes straight from Riot's own image CDN.
